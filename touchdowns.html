Anytime TD odds

TD % is the chance of at least one TD in the game. It starts from the player's expected TDs per game, pulled toward what their target volume alone would predict (so a few early red-zone looks don't dominate), blends in the last 3 games, and is scaled by the team's implied points from the betting line, compared with the week's average (). Fair odds is that probability as an American price with no vig, for comparing against a sportsbook's. Book is the best anytime-TD price across US sportsbooks; Market is the median book's implied chance, which includes the books' margin, so it runs a few points high. Edge is model minus market, in percentage points. Rec line is the receptions over/under most books offer, with the best over price. These are model estimates and a snapshot of prices, not betting advice.

Red zone & TD luck

xTD is expected receiving TDs: each target is worth the league-wide TE touchdown rate for where it was thrown from. TD − xTD above zero means the player has scored more than the usage suggests and is likely to cool off; below zero means the player has been unlucky. Red-zone splits come from ESPN play-by-play and can miss an occasional target.
