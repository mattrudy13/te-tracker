Open a game to see both teams' tight ends.

Team pts are implied by the spread and total. Model is our anytime-touchdown estimate (see Touchdowns); Book is the best anytime-TD price across US sportsbooks, with the book under it. The smaller 1st TD lines are the model's chance and the best price for scoring the game's first touchdown (by either team). Edge is model minus market in percentage points. The market's number includes the books' margin, so a small positive edge is roughly break-even. Rec/G is receptions per game. Line is the receptions over/under most books offer; O / U has the best over and under prices and their books. Tgt/G is targets per game, Share the share of the team's targets, and RZ Tgt targets inside the opponent's 20. Props usually appear midweek, so early in the week many show "–". For finished games, the box score shows the pregame TD price (✓ scored, ✗ didn't) and receptions line (Over or Under), from the last snapshot. The defense line is the opponent's TE fantasy points allowed per game (PPR), ranked 1st (fewest) to 32nd. Games in progress fill in after they end. Injury tags: Q Questionable, D Doubtful, O Out, IR injured reserve.

Data from ESPN, refreshed automatically during the season. Regular season only.
