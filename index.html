Track record

Every pick is saved at the odds snapshot and locks at kickoff, then gets graded after the game. Profit is per $100 at the listed price.

Model is our anytime-TD chance; Market is the median sportsbook's implied chance, which includes the books' margin. Edge is model minus market in percentage points. EV / $100 is the expected profit on a $100 bet at the best price if the model is right. A model that only knows targets, field position and team scoring will be wrong in ways the books aren't (injuries, game plans), so treat these as a starting point, not betting advice. Players listed as Out, Doubtful or on IR are never picked. A Q tag means Questionable. CC rows are close calls: the next-best players, shown in lighter text to fill a category to five when fewer meet its rule. They aren't official picks. They're graded in a separate shadow record, which shows whether the rules are too strict or too loose, and never count toward the official record. Grading: a pick wins if the player scores a receiving or rushing TD. A player with no box-score line is voided, the way books handle inactive players (a TE who played but drew no targets also lands there). Fades win when the player doesn't score, and have no profit figure because the "No" price isn't tracked.

Data from ESPN and The Odds API. Regular season only.
