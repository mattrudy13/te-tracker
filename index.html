Tgt share: of all the passes the team threw at a receiver in the games the player played, the percentage aimed at this player. It's the best sign of how big a role a tight end has, since ESPN doesn't publish snap counts or routes. Catch % is receptions per target. RZ Tgt counts targets inside the opponent's 20. xTD is the TDs those targets would normally produce, given where on the field they came. TD − xTD above zero means more TDs than the usage suggests (likely to cool off); below zero means fewer (due for some). TD, xTD and TD − xTD are always season totals. Rec line and Book TD are this week's receptions over/under and best anytime-TD price (no odds snapshot yet).
